The gate

How to buy

Five steps. Take your time. Only use the contract address published on this page or the official X account.

Get a Phantom wallet

Install Phantom in your browser or on your phone and write down the secret phrase offline. Never share it with anyone — not even us.

Get a little SOL

Buy SOL on an exchange you trust and withdraw it to your Phantom address on the Solana network. Leave a bit extra for fees.

Go to Pump.fun

Open Pump.fun and connect your Phantom wallet. Use the official Buy button on this site once the token is live.

Paste the contract

Copy the KINZOKU contract address from the box at the top of this page and paste it into Pump.fun. Double-check every character.

Swap SOL for KINZOKU

Choose the amount, confirm the swap in Phantom, and you are in the pack. If something looks off, do not sign.
